RAWDOG — RAdio White Dwarf catalOG

Radio evidence across white-dwarf systems.

A literature-curated catalog of radio observations and reported emission from white-dwarf systems and related candidates. Explore each system’s observations, component-specific properties and supporting evidence.

Membership includes reported detections, marginal signals and explicit upper limits. The sample is neither complete nor uniformly surveyed; overlapping classes do not establish a shared evolutionary sequence.

Columns & classification key

Distance and periods are displayed to two decimal places, WD photospheric temperature to whole kelvin, and WD mass to two decimals. Stored values and exports retain their precision. Orbital period, WD spin and radio recurrence are distinct clocks; blank spin values are not filled with recurrence or beat periods. Temperatures and masses can be model dependent. Ranges and bounds retain the catalog constraints without assigning a common confidence level. Sorting uses stored central values; missing central values, including range/limit-only entries, remain last in either direction.

CV = cataclysmic variable; mCV = magnetic-CV catalog tag, including proposed or disputed subclasses; LPT = long-period radio transient. Classes overlap. Filled symbols indicate established hosts without a flagged subclass interpretation; open symbols indicate qualified host/subclass status. Select a system for its exact classifications, host confidence and every radio observation. Radio-evidence filters retain published wording without a uniform detection threshold. Exact Gaia identifiers remain strings.
