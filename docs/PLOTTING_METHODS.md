# RAWDOG 1.0.0 plotting methods

Both publication exports and interactive foreground symbols are generated by `scripts/build.py` from `data/v1.0.0/sky_plot_input.csv` and `cmd_plot_input.csv`. CSV blanks remain missing, not zero. These source-keyed tables contain all 58 members, including excluded CMD rows and their reasons. No observation is counted as another system. Scientific check: `python scripts/check.py`.

## Sky coordinates and longitude convention

58 / 58 systems are plotted in each panel; none are excluded. 57 use the audited Gaia DR3 native ICRS position at reference epoch J2016.0. Input columns carry measurement IDs, reference keys, frame, epoch and fallback status. Coordinates propagated to epoch 2000 are not used and are not described as FK5.

CHIME/ILT J1634+44 uses Dong et al. (2025), Table 1 VLA/realfast calibrated radio localization: RA 248.6248333333°, Dec +44.8370833333° (M04513–M04514). These are **literature radio coordinates**, not a Gaia counterpart. The paper identifies J2000 but does not specify the exact reference-system realization in the audited ledger. Its detections are near MJD 60301; no proper-motion propagation is performed. For the coarse all-sky Galactic transformation we explicitly assume ICRS alignment of that reported position. We do not assert FK5 or infer a Gaia identity. Computed l=70.1706876°, b=42.5753688° agree with the paper's rounded 70.17°, 42.58° (M04521–M04522) within its printed precision. The possible reference-system difference is negligible at this figure's scale but prevents use of the fallback as precision astrometry.

Astropy `SkyCoord(..., frame='icrs').galactic` transforms exactly the selected equatorial position. The scientific check compares available audited Galactic components, including AE Aqr, and reports the maximum residual. Minor differences can follow reference-frame conventions, propagated epochs and source rounding; per-source comparison tolerance is 0.005° (18 arcsec), while the AE Aqr representative Gaia check is stricter at 0.00001° (0.036 arcsec). The fallback's published Galactic comparison uses its 0.01° printed precision. No coordinate discrepancy is silently substituted into the inputs.

Matplotlib Mollweide projection uses `x = -radians((longitude + 180) % 360 - 180)` and `y = radians(latitude)`. Zero longitude is centered, ±180° is the seam, and increasing RA or Galactic longitude runs **leftward**. RA ticks are hours modulo 24; Galactic ticks are degrees modulo 360. Tests explicitly exercise 0°, 90°, 180°, 270° and 360°. Reuses the maintained sky notebook's projection/wrapping convention; its unexplained declination shading is omitted.

## Observed Gaia CMD

56 / 58 systems are plotted. Mean Gaia G, BP and RP represent **unresolved system light, not extinction corrected**. The diagram does not isolate WD/donor/accretion light or infer intrinsic component colors. Horizontal coordinate is BP−RP; the magnitude axis increases downward (bright systems at the top).

50 foreground distances are the release-selected, evidence-linked geometric posterior values. Six additional plot-specific geometric posterior adoptions are documented below; catalog summary distances remain unselected where RC3 withheld them. No compatibility absolute magnitude, Gaia single-star pipeline estimate, photogeometric estimate or parallax inversion is automatically consumed.

These six audited records were not promoted by RC3's narrower summary-selection rules (method naming or bounds encoding). They are defensible plot-specific choices, not changes to the preferred-distance catalog. All historical and alternative distances remain in the ledger; the CMD input records the method, reference, adoption reason and uncertainty convention.

Exclusions: **CHIME/ILT J1634+44**, no secure Gaia counterpart, complete Gaia photometry or adopted foreground distance; **ASKAP J174508.9-505149**, competing parallax-posterior, photogeometric and SED distances (M04666–M04671), uncertain counterpart/low-significance parallax and no preferred scalar. Published alternatives are retained in source details. We do not resolve that modeling disagreement by arbitrarily choosing a CMD position.

`M_G = G + 5 − 5 log10(d / pc)`. Where recorded, transform distance endpoints at fixed G: `M_lower = M(G, d_upper)` and `M_upper = M(G, d_lower)`. Larger distance gives brighter/lower absolute magnitude. Posterior p16/p84 distance quantiles map to reversed magnitude endpoints; they are never converted to Gaussian errors. Some ledger endpoint conventions are unspecified (e.g. SS Cyg and V2400 Oph); those recorded bounds remain explicitly unspecified and are not assigned a confidence level. Figure vertical segments show these transformed endpoints, **not a joint photometry-distance confidence interval**. Recorded G errors stay in a separate input column. Horizontal segments use `sqrt(error_BP² + error_RP²)` only when both errors exist, assuming independence and ignoring covariance/variability. These are approximate quoted-error propagation, not guaranteed Gaussian 1σ intervals. Missing uncertainties remain blank.

Broad distance posteriors, flagged RUWE, candidate or disputed classifications and uncertain hosts carry source-specific visible qualifications in details. There is no foreground RUWE cut. All Bayesian distances depend on priors. Photometry is variable and non-simultaneous with many radio observations.

## Background product and previous work

The previous CMD PDF establishes the observed color–magnitude layout and broad marker conventions, now applied to audited membership rather than historical foreground values. Both supplied caches were inspected. The Gaia TAP cache has 212,828 rows with source_id, G, BP−RP, parallax, parallax_over_error, RUWE and distance_gspphot; the smaller VizieR cache includes coordinates and precomputed M_G. Only the larger cache is used, avoiding an undocumented union or weighting scheme.

The original query for **these caches** was not recovered. The related Rose analysis includes a `query_gaia_sample.py` selecting parallax_over_error > 5, an available extinction estimate and random_index < 10,000,000, followed by extinction-corrected aggregation. Its output schema and correction differ from the caches here; it is **not claimed as their query**. The original cache's sky coverage and historical sampling selection remain unknown. No historical query is invented.

Explicit current cuts: finite G, BP−RP, positive parallax (mas), parallax_over_error ≥10, and 0<RUWE<1.4. All 212,828 cached rows satisfy these cuts. For this high-significance field comparison alone, `M_G = G + 5 log10(parallax_mas) − 10`; no extinction correction. No zero-point or completeness correction is implied. 212,236 stars fall within the fixed density extent; 592 are outside. A 220×230 deterministic count grid spans BP−RP [−1,4.5] and M_G [−5,18]; populated-bin counts, edges and cache SHA-256 are retained. Grayscale renders log10(count+1) with a fixed normalization; density is a qualitative context layer, not a survey selection function. No individual field-star interactive points are shipped. The density CSV reproduces every figure without needing the large original cache. `scripts/background.py` can regenerate it when that cache is available.

## Classification and interactive behavior

The same palette and symbols appear in the table and both figures: blue circles for magnetic-CV tags, green squares for other CVs, purple diamonds for WD pulsars, orange triangles for long-period transients. Display priority is transient, WD pulsar, magnetic-CV tag, other CV. This priority creates one glyph per system but **does not make labels exclusive**; all audited labels remain filterable and visible. ASKAP J174508.9-505149 is an orange transient triangle with blue magnetic-CV edge. Open symbols flag non-established host or candidate/disputed/unconfirmed/proposed subclass interpretation; this flag does not classify radio detection significance. Exact subclass wording and evidence remain in details.

Filtering is opt-in for foreground symbols. Plotted and eligible counts update visibly; the field density stays fixed. Downloaded PDF/SVG/350-dpi PNG figures always show the full version. Every foreground SVG point links to its source; hover and keyboard selection carry source labels.

**CMD:** Observed unresolved Gaia BP−RP versus absolute G for 56 RAWDOG systems; not extinction corrected and not component WD magnitudes. 50 release-selected and six documented plot-specific geometric distances; two sources excluded for missing secure photometry or competing unselected distances. Vertical segments transform posterior distance endpoints at fixed G, horizontal segments approximate independent BP/RP quoted-error propagation, and G errors remain separate in the input table. Grey density provides cached high-significance Gaia field context under documented cuts; it is not a completeness model. Symbols match the sky plot; candidate/subclass and astrometry qualifications remain linked to source evidence.
